# Perfume River (Sông Hương)

## Sources
The Perfume River (Sông Hương, Hương Giang) has two sources, both in the Trường Sơn range: the left branch, Tả Trạch, rises in the Trường Đồng mountains and flows north-west, and the right branch is Hữu Trạch; they meet at Bằng Lãng Fork.

## Course
From Bằng Lãng to the Thuận An estuary the river is about 30 km long and flows very slowly, as its level is only slightly above sea level. It passes the Hòn Chén temple, Ngọc Trản mountain and the Nguyệt Biều and Lương Quán areas, then flows through Huế past Hến islet and the Sình junction, and empties into the Tam Giang lagoon.

## Name
Locals call it Sông Hương or Hương Giang. According to the source, in autumn blossoms from orchards upriver fall into the water and give it a perfume-like scent, hence the name.

## Along the banks
Thiên Mụ Pagoda stands on the river bank. Mount Ngự Bình, 105 m high and notably symmetrical, was named by Emperor Gia Long and used as a front screen for the capital. The river also passes the area of the Nguyễn emperors' tombs.

## Rainfall
The Perfume River basin has the highest rainfall in Vietnam.
