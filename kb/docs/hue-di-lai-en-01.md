# Getting to and around Huế

## By air
Phú Bài International Airport is about 15 km south of central Huế; its new terminal opened in 2023. Routes change by season, so check airline schedules.

## By rail
Huế station is on the North–South railway (Hanoi – Ho Chi Minh City); Huế and Đà Nẵng are the line's main intermediate stops.

## By road and sea
National Route 1 and the North–South expressways pass through Huế. The city's seaports are Thuận An and Chân Mây.

## In the city
The Perfume River divides central Huế into two banks; Trường Tiền Bridge links the north bank (the citadel side) with the south bank. Đông Ba Market is on the north bank near the south-east corner of the citadel.
