# Tomb of Gia Long (Thiên Thọ Mausoleum)

## Overview
The Tomb of Gia Long, officially the Thiên Thọ Mausoleum, is the tomb of Emperor Gia Long, the first Nguyễn emperor. According to the source it lies in Hương Thọ commune, Hương Trà district, about 20 km south of central Huế, and is well known for its beauty.

## Origins
It was first built for Empress Thừa Thiên, Gia Long's first wife, after her death in 1814, and later became the burial site of Gia Long and some members of the royal family.

## Layout
The main complex lies on a plain and a large hill of Thiên Thọ mount. At its centre is the double grave of Emperor Gia Long and Empress Thừa Thiên, with Minh Thành Temple, dedicated to them, on the right and Gia Long's monument on the left. The tomb of Empress Thuận Thiên (Thiên Thọ Hữu) and her Gia Thanh Temple are on the right; the temple sits at the foot of Thuận Sơn mount.

## Other graves
The site also holds the graves of many royal family members, such as Gia Long's mother (Thoại Thánh tomb) and his elder sister (Hoàng Cô tomb).
