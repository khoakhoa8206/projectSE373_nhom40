# Flag Tower of Huế (Kỳ Đài)

## Overview
The Flag Tower (Kỳ Đài) stands in the middle of the front of the Huế Citadel. It was built in 1807, the 6th year of Emperor Gia Long's reign.

## The base
Emperor Minh Mạng had the tower repaired in 1829, 1831 and 1840. Its base consists of three stacked tiers about 17.5 m high in total.

## The flagpole over time
The original wooden pole was nearly 30 m tall. In 1846 it was replaced by a pole over 32 m tall. A typhoon damaged it in 1904 and it was replaced with a cast-iron pipe, which was broken by artillery fire in 1947.

## Today
The current reinforced-concrete flagpole, erected in 1948, is 37 m tall.
