# Nine Dynastic Urns, Nine Holy Cannons and Hiển Lâm Pavilion (Huế)

## Nine Dynastic Urns
The Nine Dynastic Urns (Cửu Đỉnh) are nine bronze tripod urns commissioned by Emperor Minh Mạng in 1835 and placed in front of Thế Miếu on 1 March 1837. Each urn carries 17 motifs and one inscription. They survived the wars intact.

## Nine Holy Cannons
The Nine Holy Cannons (Cửu vị thần công) are bronze cannons cast from 31 January 1803 to December 1804 under Emperor Gia Long and named after the four seasons and the five elements. They were moved to their present position in 1917 and recognised as a National Treasure in 2012.

## Hiển Lâm Pavilion
Hiển Lâm Pavilion was built in 1821 and completed in 1822 under Emperor Minh Mạng, at the same time as Thế Miếu. At 17 m it is the tallest structure in the Imperial City.

## Thế Miếu
Thế Miếu is the temple where the Nguyễn emperors are worshipped, inside the Imperial City of Huế; the Nine Dynastic Urns stand in the courtyard in front of it.
