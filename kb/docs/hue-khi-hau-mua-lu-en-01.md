# Huế climate and the rainy/flood season

## Climate
Huế has a tropical monsoon climate. The average annual temperature is about 25 °C in the plains and hills and about 21 °C in the mountains; January averages 20 °C and July 29 °C, with extremes up to 41 °C.

## Rainy season
The rainy season runs from September to December and brings about 70% of the year's rain. Annual rainfall is about 3,200 mm: 2,500–3,500 mm in the plains and 3,000–4,500 mm in the mountains, depending on the year.

## Floods
Flooding is a major risk in Huế: the November 1999 floods killed about 600 people and affected 600,000 homes.

## Travelling in the rainy season
From September to December, follow weather forecasts and local authority notices, do not cross flooded roads and carry rain gear. In a natural disaster, call 112 for emergency help.
