# Emergency numbers (when in Huế)

## Emergency numbers
These numbers work nationwide, including in Huế:

- **113:** Police.
- **114:** Fire and rescue (fire, explosion, incidents, accidents).
- **115:** Medical emergency (ambulance).
- **112:** National line for incidents, natural disasters, catastrophes and urgent help requests; calls and messages to 112 are free of charge.

## Which number to call
- Serious injury, fainting, drowning: call **115**.
- Fire, explosion, incident or accident needing rescue: call **114**.
- Theft, threats, public disorder: call **113**.
- Natural disasters such as storms or floods, search and rescue: call **112**.

## What to say
Give your name and phone number, the location and time, what happened and how serious it is, and how many people are injured or trapped. If you do not speak Vietnamese, ask a local or site staff to call for you.

## Merging into 113
Under Decision 1166/QĐ-TTg of the Prime Minister dated 29 June 2026, numbers 113, 114 and 115 will be merged into a single nationwide emergency number, 113, following a roadmap. During the transition all three numbers 113, 114 and 115 remain in use according to the type of emergency.

## Tourist support
For help or complaints about tourism services (not emergencies), call the Huế tourism hotline 0234.3828288 published by the Huế City Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism.
