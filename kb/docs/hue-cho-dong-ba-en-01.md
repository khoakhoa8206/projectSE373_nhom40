# Đông Ba Market

## Overview
Đông Ba Market is a traditional market in Huế on Trần Hưng Đạo Road, on the north bank of the Perfume River near the south-east corner of the citadel. It covers 47,614 m², is still in use and is a landmark of the former capital.

## History
Under Emperor Gia Long the market stood just outside the citadel's Eastern Gate; a roofed building called đình Quy Giả stood at its centre, so it was also known as Quy Giả market. In 1899, as part of Huế's urban planning, Emperor Thành Thái had it moved to its present site, with 48 roofed stalls and a well. The old site later became a Franco-Vietnamese school attended by Nguyễn Tất Thành.

## Fires and rebuilding
In 1885 the market burned down during the fighting at the Huế citadel, and Emperor Đồng Khánh had it rebuilt in 1887. In 1967 it was demolished for a new building; the unfinished site was attacked during the Tết Offensive. Temporary repairs kept it running until a major renovation in 1987.

## Today
The market is usually crowded, selling fresh produce, fish, clothing, toys, shoes and handicrafts. It is open all day but busiest in the early morning.
