# Huế – city overview

## Overview
Huế is a centrally governed city in central Vietnam. It became the country's sixth centrally governed municipality in 2025, when Thừa Thiên Huế province was merged into it. Its 2024 population was 1,578,600 and its area is 4,947.11 km².

## History
Formerly known as Phú Xuân and officially renamed Huế in 1899, the city was the capital of Vietnam under the Nguyễn dynasty from 1802 to 1945. The Battle of Huế during the 1968 Tết Offensive was one of the longest and bloodiest battles of the Vietnam War. The Complex of Huế Monuments is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

## Climate
Huế has a tropical monsoon climate. The rainy season runs from September to December and brings about 70% of the year's rain, around 3,200 mm in total. January averages 20 °C and July 29 °C.

## Getting there
Phú Bài International Airport is about 15 km south of the city, Huế station is on the North–South railway, and National Route 1 and the North–South Expressway pass through.

## Food
Huế is known for spicy food and for vegetarian restaurants. Local dishes include bún bò Huế, bánh bèo, cơm hến, bánh ướt thịt nướng, bánh khoái, bánh bột lọc and bánh ít ram.
