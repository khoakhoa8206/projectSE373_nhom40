# Huế tourism hotline

## Hotline number
The Huế tourism hotline is **0234.3828288**, listed on the Support page of the visithue.vn tourism portal, whose content is the responsibility of the Huế City Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism.

## When to call
Call for tourist information or to report problems with tourism services in Huế, such as overcharging or touts. It is not an emergency number: for accidents, fire or police call 115, 114 or 113; for natural disasters call 112.

## Reporting effectively
Give the name of the business or service, the address, the time and what happened; keep receipts and photos if you have them.

## Note
Phone numbers can change; check the official visithue.vn page before calling.
