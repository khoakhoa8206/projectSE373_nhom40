# Huế Citadel (Kinh thành)

## Overview
The Huế Citadel (Kinh thành) is the outer enclosure of the Nguyễn dynasty capital, surrounding the Imperial City (Hoàng thành) and the Purple Forbidden City. It was built in the style of a Western Vauban fortress.

## Construction
Work on the citadel began in 1803 and on the imperial palace in 1804, under Emperor Gia Long. Another passage of the source dates the citadel as a whole to 1805–1832, completed under Emperor Minh Mạng.

## Walls and moat
The citadel's circumference is nearly 10 km. The walls were first built of earth and later mainly of brick, with forts placed at regular intervals along a zigzag line, together with artillery and ammunition depots. The moat is fed from the Hương (Perfume) River through sluice gates.

## Gates
The citadel has 10 main gates: 4 on the south, 2 on the north, 2 on the west and 2 on the east. Two sub-gates lead to the Trấn Bình bastion (also called Mang Cá) in the north-east corner. Two water gates on the Ngự Hà river were built in 1826 (west) and 1830 (east).

## Inside the citadel
Inside lies the Imperial City with the Meridian Gate, Thái Hòa Palace, Thế Miếu and Hiển Lâm Pavilion; the Purple Forbidden City lies behind Thái Hòa Palace. The Flag Tower (Kỳ Đài) stands in the middle of the citadel's front.
