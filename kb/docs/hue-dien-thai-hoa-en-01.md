# Thái Hòa Palace (Huế)

## Overview
Thái Hòa Palace is the main ceremonial building of the Imperial City of Huế, used for enthronements, birthday celebrations, receptions of envoys and court assemblies on the 1st and 15th days of each lunar month.

## Construction
Construction began on 21 February 1805 and finished in October 1805 on the site of the old Cần Chánh hall. In 1833 the palace was rebuilt at its present location.

## Architecture and decoration
The palace follows the traditional “trùng thiềm điệp ốc” style and its main timber is ironwood (Erythrophleum fordii). Inside are cloisonné panels bearing 197 poems and paintings in the “one poem, one painting” (nhất thi nhất họa) manner, along with gilded plaques and a gilded throne canopy.

## Coronation hall
All 13 Nguyễn emperors, from Gia Long to Bảo Đại, were crowned in Thái Hòa Palace.
