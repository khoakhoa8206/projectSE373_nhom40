# Dress code and etiquette at pagodas, royal tombs and the Imperial City (Huế)

## Scope
General guidance for visiting pagodas (such as Thiên Mụ and Từ Hiếu), the royal tombs and the Imperial City in Huế. Where a site's posted rules differ from this document, follow the rules on site.

## Dress
Wear modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees when entering pagodas, temples, tombs and royal monuments. Avoid spaghetti-strap tops and very short shorts. Carry a scarf or light jacket to cover up when needed.

## Inside the main hall
- Take off hats when entering the main hall; remove shoes if a sign asks you to or if others leave theirs outside.
- Speak quietly, do not run, and do not point at statues of worship.
- Do not sit or climb on statues, steles, railings or artefacts.

## Photography
Check signs before taking photos; some areas of worship do not allow photography. Ask before photographing monks or people at prayer.

## With children
Keep children next to an adult, remind them not to touch objects of worship, and take care on steps and stone courtyards, which are slippery when it rains.
